About Me.

Actress. Host. Author. Mama. Wife. And a whole lot of joy in one place. Here’s a little of my story.

Tabitha Brown satisfies the hunger of millions with her unique approach to veganism and her wholesome, comedic personality. From teaching people new recipes, giving a word of wisdom or enjoying a good laugh, she has become “America’s Mom” to her 14 million plus followers.

The North Carolina born, 2-time Emmy award winning host, actress, vegan food personality, mother and wife is a four-time New York Times bestselling author, multi-NAACP Image Award Winner and received her honorary doctorate from the Savannah College of Art and Design. She has acted in notable theatrical features on major networks such as ABC’s “Will and Grace” and “The Connors,” Showtime’s “The Chi,” and Netflix’s “The Upshaws.” Tabitha executive produced and hosted Food Network’s first-ever vegan cooking competition show, “It’s CompliPlated,” and co-created and hosts her own Emmy winning children’s show, “Tab Time.”

2x Emmy-winning host 4x NYT bestselling author Actress Co-founder & CEO NAACP Image Award winner

Tabitha is a serial entrepreneur, the Co-founder and CEO of her own healthy haircare line Donna’s Recipe and the Tab and Chance couples lifestyle brand. She is known for launching multi-category products in major retailers like Target, Walmart, and Ulta Beauty. In 2025, she was named the Chief Empowerment Officer for Clover, where she continued to support her passion of promoting financial wellness and empowerment for small businesses within various communities. These business endeavors and partnerships reflect her values, emphasizing health, kindness, and community, because that’s her business!

Above all, Tabitha teaches us about spreading love, having faith, perseverance, paving a unique path to achieving dreams, and being one’s authentic self.
