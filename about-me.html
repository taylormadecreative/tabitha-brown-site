About Me.

Actress. Host. Author. Mama. Wife. And a whole lot of joy in one place. Here’s a little of my story.

I’m Tabitha Brown, and I believe the world is a better place when we lead with love. I got my start as an actress, but the world came to know me through short videos in my kitchen, cooking plant-based food and talking to folks like family. What began as a way to feel better in my own body turned into a calling: reminding people to be encouraged, to have a good day, and if they can’t, to not go messing up nobody else’s.

The work

I went vegan after years of chronic pain and fatigue, and finding my way back to feeling good changed everything. These days I get to do the things I love out loud: I’m a three-time New York Times bestselling author, an Emmy-winning host, an actress, and a multiple NAACP Image Award winner. I even received an honorary doctorate along the way. Every bit of it comes from the same place: telling the truth and spreading a little sunshine.

The family

None of it happens without my family. My husband Chance and I built a life and a brand on love, and we love bringing you along for the ride. Our children keep me grounded, and my faith keeps me going. Whether it’s hair care, a children’s show, a fragrance, or a brand new body mist, everything I make is really about the same thing: helping you feel seen, loved, and heard.

The heart

People ask how I keep so much going. The honest answer is that it’s all one thing to me. It’s all love. So look around, honey. Everything here is a door to something I made with my whole heart, and I’m so glad you came to visit.
